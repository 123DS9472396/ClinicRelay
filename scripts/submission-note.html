CLINICRELAY / AFTER-HOURS PATIENT RECOVERY IMPLEMENTATION NOTE 01 / 01
THE PROPOSAL
Catch the call.
Keep the patient.
A simple after-hours receptionist workflow that turns missed opportunities into an organised, consent-aware callback queue. | Prepared by Dipesh Sharma
THE PROBLEM
A Mumbai dental clinic reports that around 30% of calls are lost outside business hours. The owner is non-technical and the monthly operating budget is INR 5,000. A missed call can become a lost appointment when no one captures the patient's need or contact details.
WHAT THE PROTOTYPE DOES
  • Guides a patient through intent, name, valid Indian mobile number, preferred time and callback consent.
  • Creates a structured enquiry and call record; appointment requests are stored as Requested, not auto-confirmed.
  • Gives the clinic a searchable inbox, priority flags, callback notes, status updates and simple performance metrics.
  • Uses approved clinic facts for hours/location; urgent or symptom-related messages are flagged for human review.
WHAT I REJECTED
  • Standalone website chatbot: it does not, by itself, recover calls made to the clinic telephone number.
  • Automatic booking: no live dentist calendar is connected, so a human must verify availability.
  • AI diagnosis: outside a receptionist's role and creates clinical risk.
  • Full practice-management suite: unnecessary scope before the call workflow and demand are validated.
THE CHOICE
Build ClinicRelay: a guided after-hours receptionist, a call/enquiry inbox, appointment requests and a human callback workflow. Keep the owner's next action obvious: review, contact, then confirm.
IMPLEMENTATION

Prototype stack: Node.js server, lightweight responsive web UI, validated intake workflow, local persistent demo store and optional server-side Groq intent classification. The app runs without external credentials.

Production path: connect the clinic's existing number through a verified provider/forwarding setup; accept signed, idempotent call events; move records to secured PostgreSQL; add admin authentication, tenant controls and audit logs.

INR 5,000 / MONTH BUDGET

Model monthly spend as number rental + connected minutes × provider rate + AI usage + hosting + messaging + taxes. Confirm live rate cards and inbound routing before choosing a provider. Start with a low-volume pilot, usage caps and 70/85/95% spend alerts; retain a callback fallback if the cap is reached. The local prototype has no per-minute voice charge.

RISKS & SAFEGUARDS
  • Speech recognition can be incomplete; validate contact details and provide a human fallback.
  • Use synthetic data in the demo. Live use needs a patient notice/consent design, access controls, retention rules and privacy/legal review.
  • Do not send patient health information to an AI provider until vendor data handling and legal basis are reviewed.
WHAT I WOULD DO NEXT

Pilot on a non-critical line; measure eligible after-hours calls, captured enquiries, completed callbacks and confirmed appointments; review the first provider invoice against the budget; then refine FAQs and approved Hindi/Marathi flows with clinic staff.

PROTOTYPE BOUNDARY ClinicRelay demonstrates the enquiry workflow with simulated calls and fictional records. It does not yet answer, forward or place a live telephone call. No real clinic performance is claimed.
